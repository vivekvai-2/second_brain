## How to Read This Document

Each entity is assessed across four dimensions:
- **Value proposition to crystallise** — what your distinctive offer is *in their context*
- **Assignment types** — what they will actually pay for
- **Phase 0 realistic?** — whether entry is viable before May 2027 without prior publications
- **Annual value potential** — indicative revenue once the relationship is established

Three strategic themes anchor all value propositions:
1. **AI Risk Stack** — cross-regulator enforcement logic for DPDP + CERT-In + RBI + SEBI
2. **DPI + AI Governance** — accountability mapping for Aadhaar, DigiLocker, ONDC
3. **Meta-Governance Gaps** — compute sovereignty, cross-border liability, enforcement gaps
