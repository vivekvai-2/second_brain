## Differentiator

### Market positioning

Current landscape:

- Think tanks such as Esya and Takshashila publish policy analysis but do not operationalise it for practitioners. [file:1]
- Consultants such as Judge India and Coralesecure implement frameworks but do not publish openly. [file:1]
- Academic centres such as CeRAI advise the government, but outputs remain closed-door. [file:1]
- Large think tanks such as Vidhi and CSTEP work with the government but lack AI-specific practitioner depth. [file:1]

### White space

India's only public-facing institution bridging academic rigour with implementation practicality, producing frameworks that policymakers cite and enterprises actually deploy. [file:1]

### Distinctive claim

Cross-regulator enforcement logic translator: synthesising how DPDP, CERT-In, RBI, SEBI, IRDAI, and TRAI interact for AI systems, and converting regulatory silos into unified assurance frameworks. [file:1]

## Derivative content architecture

- 4–6 LinkedIn carousels that break the work into practitioner-digestible frameworks. [file:1]
- 2–3 consultation responses applying the theme logic to regulatory drafts. [file:1]
- 1 regulatory learning note consolidating cross-consultation insights. [file:1]
- 3–5 expert LinkedIn comments on related policy discussions. [file:1]

## Operating system

### The authority flywheel

**Consultation → Learning Note → Carousel Loop** [file:1]

1. Draft a regulatory learning note in 1.5–2 hours using a 5-question template focused on the regulator's real problem, political and operational worries, blind spots, and industry misunderstandings. [file:1]
2. Draft the consultation response in 8–20 hours, either medium-depth (8–10 hours, 12–18 pages) or deep (15–20 hours, 20–30 pages with frameworks), anchored in regulatory text and comparative global references. [file:1]
3. Extract carousel insights in 3–4 hours by identifying 1–2 novel operational insights, creating a 7–10 slide hero carousel, and writing a caption that follows Hook → India → Operational → Debate. [file:1]
4. Publish within 7 days of filing by posting the carousel, linking to the public submission version, seeding the first comment, and tagging relevant experts or think tanks rather than regulators. [file:1]
5. Follow up after 2 weeks with the regulator policy team using a short note, offer a 15-minute implementation call, and track the response in a validation milestone tracker. [file:1]
