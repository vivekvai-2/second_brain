# Integration Note: Harmonisation Between Source Documents

This integrated document merges two planning artefacts developed approximately three months apart. The analysis below identifies where the documents are consistent, where harmonisation was required, and what that means for execution.

## 1. What is Consistent

The two documents share a common strategic logic and require no structural reconciliation on the following dimensions:

- **End-state vision:** Both converge on a boutique India-first AI Governance institution generating ₹3.5–5 crore annually by 2030, funded 40% from foundations, 25% government contracts, 20% implementation, 15% capacity building.
- **Three strategic themes:** AI Risk Stack, DPI + AI Governance, and Meta-Governance Gaps are consistently referenced as the anchor framework in both documents.
- **Phase 0 definition:** Both documents treat May 2027 as the validation gate requiring 2 confirmation signals (regulator + foundation) and 3 anchor publications.
- **Overloaded quarters:** Both documents implicitly recognise Q3–Q4 2026 as high-risk bandwidth periods requiring kill-switch discipline.

## 2. What Required Harmonisation

The following tensions were identified and resolved in this integrated document:

> **TENSION 1 — Sequencing language:** Consultancy-V2 uses Phase 0/Phase 1 with quarterly labels. Partner Map uses Immediate / Q4 2026 / Post-Phase 0 / Post-PhD. These have been unified into a single four-horizon model in Section 3.

> **TENSION 2 — PhD gate visibility:** Consultancy-V2 does not explicitly flag which actions require doctoral completion. Partner Map is explicit for some entities (ICRIER, GovAI, IDRC, RBI) but inconsistent across others. Section 5 provides a full PhD-gate classification across all 40+ partner entities.

> **TENSION 3 — Revenue aggregation gap:** Consultancy-V2 projects ₹3.5–5 crore by 2030. Partner Map provides per-entity ranges but these were never summed. Aggregating the conservative floor of all 'Phase 0 realistic: Yes' entities yields ₹85–165 lakh annually at steady state — consistent with the 2030 target once post-PhD and foundation streams are added.

> **TENSION 4 — Content production demand:** Consultancy-V2 budgets 40 carousels over 18 months. Partner Map generates derivative content demand from 40+ relationships, some of which (DSCI, NASSCOM, IndiaAI) carry independent publication obligations. The integrated content calendar in Section 4 reconciles these against the 624–780-hour available bandwidth.

> **TENSION 5 — Anchor paper nomenclature:** Consultancy-V2 labels publications by theme (AI Risk Stack, DPI Governance, Meta-Governance). Partner Map references P1/P2/P3 papers. In this document P1 = AI Risk Stack brief (Q3 2026), P2 = DPI Governance (Q4 2026–Q1 2027), P3 = Meta-Governance Gaps (Q1 2027).
