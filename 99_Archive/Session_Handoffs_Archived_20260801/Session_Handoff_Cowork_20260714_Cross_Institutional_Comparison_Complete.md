## 2. Key Analytical & Conceptual Findings

1. **Deontic Bifurcation:** We defined and extended the concept of [[Regulatory_Parallelism]] by introducing *Deontic Bifurcation*—the structural coexistence of highly coercive, binding sectoral "shall" rules alongside flexible, principles-based horizontal "should" guidelines, which systematically drives enterprises to prioritize rigid compliance over ethical principles.
2. **Assurance Reuse Gap:** We grounded the concept of [[Governance_Debt]] by documenting the *Assurance Reuse Gap*—the systematic inability of enterprises to reuse technical audit artifacts across different regulatory forums, which multiplies "governance theatre" without improving substantive system security.
3. **Infrastructure vs. Substantive Coherence:** We refined [[Institutional_Coherence]] by distinguishing *horizontal infrastructure coherence* (which CERT-In achieves by standardizing the cyber-auditor market via its empanelment scheme) from *vertical substantive incoherence* (where RBI, SEBI, and MeitY maintain parallel, non-aligned rules sitting atop that shared infrastructure).
4. **The DLT-Privacy Conflict (Direct Legal Contradiction):** We identified a direct legal contradiction between TRAI's UCC Spam Direction 2026 (mandating real-time DLT-based sharing of flagged spam numbers) and MeitY's DPDP Rules 2025 (mandating a strict right to erasure), as DLT architectures are structurally immutable.
