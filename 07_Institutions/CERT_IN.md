# CERT-In

**Type:** Institution **Status:** Active **Last Updated:** 2026-06-12

---

## Core Synthesis

The Indian Computer Emergency Response Team is the national cybersecurity incident response and coordination body under MeitY — the third regulatory actor in the P3 corpus alongside RBI and SEBI, and the only actor among the three that demonstrates cross-regulator referencing behaviour in the coordination-pair analysis. Three SEBI→CERT-In references appear in the P3 corpus; zero RBI→CERT-In cross-references are found. This asymmetry is analytically significant: it establishes that SEBI is institutionally capable of cross-referencing other regulators and chooses to do so for cybersecurity but not for AI governance coordination with RBI. CERT-In is also the immediate consulting opportunity — empanelment is Phase 0 accessible without a publication gate.

## Institutional Profile

- **Type:** National nodal agency (statutory authority under IT Act 2000, Section 70B)
- **Established:** 2004
- **Governing statute:** IT Act 2000; CERT-In Directions 2022 (mandatory incident reporting)
- **Primary mandate:** Cybersecurity incident prevention, response, and coordination; vulnerability disclosure; cyber forensics; threat intelligence sharing
- **AI-relevant jurisdiction:** Any organisation operating critical information infrastructure or regulated financial/government IT systems; incident reporting covers AI-related cybersecurity failures
- **Enforcement mechanism:** Mandatory incident reporting (6-hour window for material incidents); penalties for non-compliance; empanelment authority for cybersecurity auditors

## Institutional Logic (Research Relevance)

CERT-In's governing logic is **incident-response and resilience** — its frame is reactive (after failure) rather than preventive (before deployment). This produces an AI governance contribution focused narrowly on security incidents involving AI systems, not on pre-deployment risk assessment or cross-regulator coordination. In the P3 corpus, CERT-In functions as a downstream partner for cybersecurity-specific AI obligations rather than as a co-regulator of AI governance. The three SEBI→CERT-In references in the corpus reflect this: SEBI references CERT-In for cybersecurity incident notification, not for AI governance principles. This makes CERT-In a supporting actor in the PRIS research narrative — present in the corpus but not a protagonist in the Regulatory Parallelism construct.
