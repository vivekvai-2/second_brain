## Core Synthesis

The National Cyber and AI Center (NCAIC) is a **provisional institution entry** in the PRIS corpus, pending confirmation of its institutional identity, statutory status, and formal Government of India recognition. NCAIC entered the corpus through NCAIC_1 (AI Governance Framework for India 2025-26, Sept 2025), a whitepaper describing a proposed cross-sectoral coordinating body for India's AI governance architecture. As a corpus-relevant institution, NCAIC's principal analytical role is as the **proposed-but-un-enacted coordination layer** contrasting with CERT-In's operative sector-by-sector jurisdictional expansion — the clearest "capacity-form without capacity-function" pair in the Indian AI governance architecture, and a direct fsQCA Structural Readiness (SR) condition indicator.

## Institutional Logic (P3/P4 Analytical Relevance)

NCAIC's proposed governing logic is **coordination through synthesis** — bridging existing sectoral instruments (DPDP Act, CERT-In Directions, sectoral AI circulars) rather than issuing binding rules of its own. This positions NCAIC as an aspirational Institutional Coherence mechanism at the meta-regulatory layer: a proposed coordinator without the statutory preemption authority the US AI Litigation Task Force (International_5) has, or the operative cross-agency framework the UK FPC/PRA/FCA/FSDC architecture (International_10) offers. Contrasted with CERT-In's operative sector-by-sector jurisdictional expansion (CERT_In_1 audit mandate; CERT_In_2 SatCom framework), NCAIC illustrates the pattern *proposed coordination + operative sectoral capacity* — a two-layer configuration where sectoral capacity is being built while coordination remains at the proposal stage. For fsQCA SR calibration, NCAIC's un-enacted status contributes 0 to SR at the coordination-layer dimension.
