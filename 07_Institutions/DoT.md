# DoT — Department of Telecommunications

**Type:** Institution **Status:** Active **Created:** 2026-08-02 (missing-institutions sourcing pass)

---

## Institutional Identity

Department of Telecommunications, Ministry of Communications. The **rule-making** authority under the Telecommunications Act 2023, distinct from [[TRAI]], which is the recommending and regulating authority. The [[Telecom_AI_Governance]] domain was built with TRAI as its only institutional anchor; DoT is the body that would convert TRAI's recommendations into binding rules, and its absence from the vault was a structural gap.

**Standard-setting authority:** telecommunication services, telecommunication networks and equipment.

## The Telecommunications Act 2023

- **Instrument:** Act No. 44 of 2023, assented **24 December 2023**; replaces telegraph-era legislation
- **Gazette:** https://egazette.gov.in/WriteReadData/2023/250880.pdf
- **India Code:** https://www.indiacode.nic.in/handle/123456789/20101?view_type=browse
- **Commencement:** phased. First tranche of selected provisions effective **26 June 2024**
- **Rule-making expected:** approximately **35–37 Rules**; initial estimates suggested at least six months before substantive notification began

## The Central Finding — no AI anywhere

**No DoT notification, advisory or consultation paper referencing AI, automated systems or algorithmic decision-making was located.** Recorded as a confirmed absence, not inferred from silence. DoT's rule-making under the Act to date concerns spectrum assignment, right-of-way, interception and security provisions, and standards/conformity. None of the retrieved rule-notification analyses reference AI or algorithmic systems as a distinct regulatory subject.

**This is the third independent confirmation of the same telecom finding.** [[Telecom_AI_Governance]] already records: (1) TRAI's July 2023 cross-sector AI Recommendations produced no binding instrument, and DoT's own June 2026 Telecommunications Act rule-making contains no AI references; (2) [[DEPA]]'s 2020 claim that the consent architecture was "being piloted in the health and telecom sectors" produced a health instantiation and nothing in telecom. This pass confirms it from the rule-maker's side. **Three unrealised telecom announcements, from three directions, across six years.** The domain's "institutional vision exceeding institutional reach" finding is now very well supported.

## Open Questions

- **OQ-DOT-01:** A complete itemised inventory of Rules notified under the Act to date — distinguishing covered from uncovered subject areas — **was not compiled**. DoT's official notifications index returned no consolidated list in this search. A dedicated follow-up query targeting dot.gov.in specifically is the obvious next step, and would let the "no AI" finding be stated exhaustively rather than as a search result.
- **OQ-DOT-02:** Will telecom operators designated Significant Data Fiduciaries under DPDP be reached by Rule 4 Consent Manager obligations from 13 November 2026 — delivering by horizontal statute what sectoral policy twice failed to deliver? Note the [[DPB]] dependency: an unstaffed Board cannot register Consent Managers.
