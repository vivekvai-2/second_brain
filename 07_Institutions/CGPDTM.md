## **Institutional Identity**

The Office of the Controller General of Patents, Designs and Trade Marks (O/o CGPDTM), commonly the Indian Patent Office (IPO) in its patent capacity, is the subordinate office of [[07_Institutions/DPIIT]] that administers the Patents Act, 1970, the Designs Act, 2000, the Trade Marks Act, 1999, and the Geographical Indications of Goods Act, 1999. It is **distinct from DPIIT itself**: DPIIT sets IPR policy, CGPDTM exercises the statutory and quasi-judicial examination and grant functions.

That distinction matters for this corpus. [[07_Institutions/Corpus/DPIIT/DPIIT_1]] is a **policy consultation** on generative AI and copyright — DPIIT acting as policymaker. [[07_Institutions/Corpus/CGPDTM/CGPDTM_1_AI_in_Patent_Examination_Guidelines_2026]] is an **internal operating instruction** on AI use by examiners and controllers — CGPDTM acting as an administrator of its own AI deployment. Two bodies, one ministry, two different governance modes, no cross-citation between them.

**Key governance domains:**
- Patent examination and grant (Patents Act, 1970)
- Designs, trade marks, and geographical indications registration
- Quasi-judicial determination in opposition and revocation proceedings
- Since 2026: internal governance of AI use in examination functions

## **Position in the AI Governance Landscape**

CGPDTM enters this corpus as an instance of the **state-as-AI-user** pattern rather than the state-as-AI-regulator pattern that dominates the rest of the corpus. Its instrument binds its own officers, not regulated entities.
