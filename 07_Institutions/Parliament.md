# Parliament of India — Standing Committee on Communications and Information Technology

**Type:** Institution Note **Status:** Stub — created 2026-08-02 during DPDP thin-node intake **Last Updated:** 2026-08-02

---

## Core Synthesis

Parliamentary standing committees sit outside the executive-regulator institutions (RBI, SEBI, MeitY, IRDAI) that make up most of this corpus, but they are a distinct evidentiary source for legislative history and pre-enactment institutional design reasoning — relevant wherever PRIS analyses *why* a statute took the shape it did, not just what the enacted text says. This stub exists to house the Standing Committee on Communications and Information Technology, whose 48th Report ("Citizens' Data Security and Privacy," 2022–23) is the only pre-enactment legislative-history document currently coded in the corpus for the DPDP Act.

**Institutional note:** the DPDP Bill, 2023 itself was passed without being referred to this Committee — the 48th Report examined the earlier 2022 draft framework, not the final Act. This is an important limitation flagged in the corpus node, not a citation to be used loosely as "Parliament's committee reviewed the DPDP Act."
