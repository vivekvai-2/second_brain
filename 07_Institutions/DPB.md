# DPB — Data Protection Board of India

**Type:** Institution **Status:** Active (as a node) — **the institution itself is constituted but unstaffed** **Created:** 2026-08-02 (missing-institutions sourcing pass)

---

## Institutional Identity

- **Constitutive basis:** Section 18, Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (22 of 2023)
- **Procedural rules:** DPDP Rules 2025, **Rules 17–21** — constitution, procedure, terms of service. In force from **13 November 2025**, the date of gazette notification, ahead of every substantive obligation in the Rules
- **Design:** "a body corporate with the mandate to function as an independent adjudicatory authority for the effective enforcement of the provisions of the Act," envisaged as **digital-by-design**
- **Composition:** Chairperson + 4 Members. Section 19(3) requires at least one Member with legal expertise; others to hold domain knowledge in data governance, dispute resolution, technology or the digital economy
- **Terms of service:** DPDP Act 2023 and the **Fifth Schedule** of the DPDP Rules 2025

## The Central Finding — an adjudicatory authority with no adjudicators

**As of 31 July 2026 the Board has no appointed Chairperson and no appointed Members. It has issued no orders, no directions, and no substantive adjudicatory statements.** LiveLaw's assessment: "It exists, in the strict sense, only as a statutory possibility."

Timeline:

| Date | Event |
|---|---|
| 24 Dec 2023 | DPDP Act assented; s.18 creates the Board |
| **13 Nov 2025** | Rules 17–21 in force — the Board's constitution and procedure are law |
| **6 May 2026** | MeitY invites applications (F. No. 2(1)/2026-Pers.I): 1 Chairperson, 4 Members |
| 6 Jun 2026 | Further notification continuing the appointment process |
| **31 Jul 2026** | **Still unstaffed.** Search-cum-Selection Committees — Cabinet Secretary chairs the Chairperson committee, MeitY Secretary the Members committee — "appear to remain at the stage of soliciting names rather than finalising them" |

Appointment terms (from the 6 May 2026 notification): Chairperson must have held a post equivalent to Additional Secretary to the Government of India or above; minimum age 55; tenure not exceeding 2 years or until age 65; consolidated monthly salary ₹4,50,000 (Chairperson) and ₹4,00,000 (Members), no house or car; one-year post-office employment restriction.

**Backend built, front end empty.** IT Secretary S. Krishnan confirmed (PTI, reported 28 Dec 2025) that "the requisite software for a digital-first office of the body has already been developed" and the Board would be operational "in the coming months," declining a timeline. **No dedicated official DPBI website was located.**

## ⚠ The Dependency Nobody Has Flagged

**DPDP Rules 2025 Rule 4 — Consent Manager registration — commences 13 November 2026.** Registration is with **the Board**. A Board with no Chairperson and no Members cannot register anyone.

The corpus already records ([[DEPA]], [[07_Institutions/Corpus/MeitY/MeitY_4_DPDP_Rules_2025]]) that India will then have two consent-manager regimes: the operative RBI Account Aggregator regime, and the statutory DPDP regime that is "enacted but not operative." **This node supplies the reason the second one may stay inoperative past its own commencement date** — not drafting delay, but an unstaffed registrar. Roughly three months from the date of this node.

The same dependency runs through every substantive obligation commencing **13 May 2027** (Rules 3, 5–16, 22, 23 — breach notification, security safeguards, children's data, SDF obligations, Data Principal rights, cross-border transfer). All are enforced by the Board. **India's data-protection enforcement architecture currently has no enforcer.**

## Analytical Significance

**A new sequencing variant: constituted-but-unstaffed.** The corpus records four instances of "institution before regulation" — CSIRT-Power operational before its formalising regulation; the AA Master Directions preceding DEPA by four years; [[Sahamati]] operating years before SRO recognition; [[BIS]] adopting a standard and training auditors before any accreditation scheme. This is the inverse and it is worse: **the regulation exists, the institution is constituted in law, the software is built, and there is nobody in the chair.** Suggest recording as a distinct pattern — *institutional shell* — in [[Governance_Debt]], because unlike the others it produces no governance capacity at all, only its appearance.

**It also sharpens [[Contestability_Redress]].** That note records DPDP's absence of any ADM provision as India's statutory contestability gap. This adds a second, independent layer: even the rights DPDP *does* create have no forum. A data principal today has statutory rights, a notified procedure for complaining to the Board, and no Board to complain to.

**And it qualifies the [[Legitimacy]] research gap.** That note flags that no legitimacy-perception study exists for any Indian AI or DPI regulator. The DPB is a rare natural case: an institution whose legitimacy can be studied *before* it has taken a single decision.
