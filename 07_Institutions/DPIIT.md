## **Institutional Identity**

The Department for Promotion of Industry and Internal Trade (DPIIT) operates under the Ministry of Commerce and Industry (MoCI). It is the nodal department for industrial policy, FDI policy, the Startup India programme, intellectual property rights policy, and industrial licencing. DPIIT is **distinct from MeitY** (Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology, under the Ministry of Electronics & IT / MoE&IT), which governs digital infrastructure, data protection, and AI governance frameworks. The two ministries sit under different Cabinet portfolios and do not share a statutory coordination mechanism.

**Key governance domains:**
- Intellectual property policy (Patents Act, Copyright Act, Trade Marks Act)
- FDI policy and industrial licencing
- Startup India and innovation ecosystem development
- Competition and investment facilitation

## **PRIS Research Relevance**

DPIIT enters the PRIS corpus through its December 2025 Working Paper on GenAI and Copyright (DPIIT_1), which proposes a mandatory blanket-licensing mechanism (CRCAT) for AI training data. This opens a **third AI governance track** operating entirely outside and without cross-reference to the SEBI/RBI/MeitY financial-and-data regulatory stacks.

The significance for PRIS research is structural: the CRCAT proposal addresses AI training-data acquisition without engaging:
- DPDP Rules 2025's consent or localisation framework (MeitY_1)
- RBI FREE-AI's model-governance obligations (RBI_FREE_AI_2025)
- MeitY's AI ethics principles (MeitY_2)

This confirms that India's AI governance fragmentation is not limited to the BFSI domain (SEBI–RBI parallelism) or the data-AI interface (MeitY–RBI) but extends into the IP governance layer — a distinct Ministry of Commerce track operating in parallel to Ministry of Electronics & IT's AI governance infrastructure.
