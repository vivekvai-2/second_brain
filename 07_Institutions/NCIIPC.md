# NCIIPC — National Critical Information Infrastructure Protection Centre

**Type:** Institution **Status:** Active (thin) — created 2026-08-02 during Power Grid/Critical Infrastructure domain build **Last Updated:** 2026-08-02

---

## Core Synthesis

NCIIPC, housed within the National Technical Research Organisation (NTRO), is India's designated nodal agency for Critical Information Infrastructure (CII) protection across all designated Critical Sectors, including Power & Energy. Its published guidance — the Guidelines for Protection of NCII (Version 2.0, 2015) and a Forty Critical Controls document — is cross-sectoral and general (asset management, incident response, access control), not power-specific and not AI-specific. NCIIPC's role in the power-sector cyber-governance architecture is as the **reporting destination** for CII assets identified under CEA's 2021 Guidelines, not as an independent source of power-sector or AI-specific rules. As of this note, NCIIPC has issued no document addressing AI-specific risks (model integrity, adversarial ML, algorithmic decision accountability) in any sector.

## Institutional Profile

- **Type:** Statutory nodal agency, housed within NTRO
- **Governing statute:** Section 70A(1), Information Technology (Amendment) Act, 2008
- **Primary mandate:** Identification and protection of Critical Information Infrastructure across designated Critical Sectors (Power & Energy, Banking & Finance, Telecom, Transport, Government, Strategic & Public Enterprises)
- **AI-relevant jurisdiction:** All CII-designated entities, cross-sectorally — not power-specific
- **Key published guidance:** Guidelines for Protection of NCII v2.0 (16 January 2015); Forty Critical Controls (undated in this intake — flagged for verification); broader catalog per its own listing includes Cyber Security Audit Baseline Requirements, SOP for Incident Response, SOP for Audit of CII/Protected Systems, Rules for Information Security Practices for Protected Systems, National Cyber Security Policy 2013 — none confirmed as power-specific or AI-specific by title

## Institutional Logic (Research Relevance)

NCIIPC's logic is **cross-sectoral CII baseline-setting**, not sector-specific technical regulation — the inverse emphasis to CEA, which is power-specific but (per its own instruments) purely cybersecurity-scoped. Together, CEA and NCIIPC form a two-tier structure (sector-specific technical rules + cross-sectoral CII reporting/baseline) with **no AI-specific layer at either tier** — the confirmed gap this domain documents.
