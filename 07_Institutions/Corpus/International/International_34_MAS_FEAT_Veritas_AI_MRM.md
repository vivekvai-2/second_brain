# International_34 — MAS FEAT Principles, Veritas Initiative, and AI Model Risk Management (2018–2024)

**Framework:** [[Singapore_AI_Governance]] | **Added:** 2026-08-02 (Singapore sourcing pass)

Consolidated node — one regulator, one continuous programme across six years, per the PRIS evidence-reuse threshold.

---

## Document Identity

**(a) FEAT Principles** — *Principles to Promote Fairness, Ethics, Accountability and Transparency in the Use of Artificial Intelligence and Data Analytics in Singapore's Financial Sector*
MAS, with an industry working group. **November 2018.**
Link: https://www.mas.gov.sg/~/media/MAS/News%20and%20Publications/Monographs%20and%20Information%20Papers/FEAT%20Principles%20Final.pdf
Structure: **14 principles** — Fairness (Justifiability 1–2; Accuracy and Bias 3–4), Ethics (5–6), Accountability (Internal 7–9; External 10–11), Transparency (12–14).
Status: **Advisory. Never formally revised.** Not a Notice or Directive.

**(b) Veritas Initiative** — MAS-led industry consortium. Index: https://www.mas.gov.sg/schemes-and-initiatives/veritas

| Phase | Concluded | Deliverables |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 6 Jan 2021 | Doc 1 (Fairness assessment methodology), Doc 2 (case studies). Credit risk scoring + customer marketing. Open-source code on GitHub |
| 2 | 4 Feb 2022 | Doc 3 (full FEAT methodology), **3A Fairness / 3B Ethics & Accountability / 3C Transparency**, Doc 4 (case studies), **Toolkit v1.0**. 27 companies |
| 3 | 26 Jun 2023 | Doc 5 (*From Methodologies to Integration*), Doc 6 (case studies), **Toolkit v2.0** covering Ethics/Accountability/Transparency. 31 players; leads Accenture and Bank of China; companion whitepaper on seven FIs' integration experience |

Toolkit: https://github.com/veritas-toolkit/
Doc 6: https://www.mas.gov.sg/-/media/mas/news/media-releases/veritas-document-6---feat-principles-assessment-case-studies.pdf
Status: **Phase 3 is final — the Initiative has not been extended.** No phase supersedes another; each extended scope.

**(c) AI Model Risk Management** Information Paper. MAS, **5 December 2024**.
Link: https://www.mas.gov.sg/publications/monographs-or-information-paper/2024/artificial-intelligence-model-risk-management
Basis: mid-2024 thematic review of banks' AI/GenAI model risk practices. Covers AI Governance and Oversight; Key Risk Management Systems and Processes; Development and Deployment. Explicitly builds on and cross-references FEAT.
Status: **Advisory** — an "Information Paper" documenting observed good practice. MAS *encourages* reference; does not mandate.

## Analytical Significance

**Methodology without mandate.** MAS has produced, over six years, the most operationally developed AI assessment apparatus in the corpus — per-principle assessment methodologies, two open-source toolkit releases, documented multi-institution pilots — and has attached **no binding obligation to any of it**. Bindingness, to the extent it exists, is generated by the supervisory relationship rather than by instrument text. For [[Deontic_Bifurcation]] this is a fourth type: not mandatory-core-plus-advisory-annexure, not designed-vs-emergent, not delegated-across-documents, but **supervisory-expectation bindingness** with no deontic in the text at all.

**The direct comparison that matters for [[P3_BFSI_JEIM]] and [[P4_Doctrinal_IJLIT]].** RBI's FREE-AI report is a Committee report with recommendations and no operative amendment; MAS's AI MRM paper is an Information Paper with good practices and no mandate. **Structurally these are the same instrument type.** But RBI *also* issues binding Master Directions on adjacent matters and FREE-AI recommends amending seven of them; MAS has no binding AI instrument at any level. On binding force India is ahead of Singapore, which inverts the corpus's default framing. What India lacks is not Singapore's rules — it is Singapore's **methodology**: nothing in the Indian corpus resembles Veritas Doc 3A/3B/3C.

**Measurement value for D4.** Documents 3A, 3B and 3C are assessment methodologies for Fairness, for Ethics and Accountability, and for Transparency — assessing the *deploying institution's process*, not a human subject's perception of an explanation. The EA-principle notes created earlier today recorded that available instruments (SCS, XAI Trust Scale, CAS) are subject-perception scales and cannot be applied to a governance regime without category error. Veritas is the missing instrument class and should be evaluated as a third D4 calibration option. See [[fsQCA]].

**Staleness.** Phase 3 closed June 2023. GenAI and agentic systems arrived after. The 2024 AI MRM paper addresses the gap from outside the Veritas structure rather than by extending it — an instance of a methodology programme that stopped being maintained while the technology moved, which is [[Governance_Debt]] in an unusual form: not an unimplemented rule but an unmaintained method.

## Coding Notes (IG 2.0)

High-value control case. FEAT's 14 principles are almost entirely **ADIC (norms) without Or-else** — a full national sectoral regime with no enforceable deontic. Coding FEAT against RBI FREE-AI gives a clean cross-jurisdiction deontic comparison holding sector and period roughly constant. The AI MRM paper's "good practices" framing should be coded as Shared Strategies (AIC) rather than Norms where no deontic verb appears at all.
