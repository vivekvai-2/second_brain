# fsQCA 20-Case Shortlist Registry

**Source:** fsqca_SOM_V2.docx Table S7 — read and verified 2026-06-12
**Purpose:** Master registry of the 20-case AI governance maturity shortlist from which the 10 fsQCA cases are selected (OQ-09: OVERDUE since 31 May 2026)
**Nature of cases:** National AI governance strategies and frameworks — NOT P3 BFSI regulatory instruments
**Maturity scoring:** Legal(0-2) + Enforce(0-2) + Cover(0-2) + Time(0-2) = 0–8 total
**Tiers:** Emerging (0–3) | Intermediate (4–6) | Advanced (7–8)

---

## Scoring Criteria

| Criterion | 0 | 1 | 2 |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Legal** | Draft/voluntary | Formally adopted, non-binding | Legally binding statute/regulation |
| **Enforce** | None | Planned/pilot | Operational (audits, sandboxes, penalties) |
| **Cover** | Single sector | Multiple but partial | Multi-sectoral, economy-wide |
| **Time** | <1 year | 1–2 years | >2 years |

## fsQCA Condition and Calibration Architecture

*Source: fsqca_SOM_V2 Tables S3–S6*

**Four causal conditions:**

| Condition | Code | What it measures |
|---|---|---|
| Ethical Alignment | EA | Translation of ethical principles into enforceable governance instruments |
| Stakeholder Role Clarity | SR | Distribution of roles/authority among state, market, and civil society actors |
| Regulatory Logic | RL | Underlying design rationale (risk-based, ethics-driven, innovation-oriented) |
| Stakeholder Inclusion | SI | Nature, timing, and scope of stakeholder engagement |
| **Outcome** | **Legitimacy** | D3 open — pending TAC operationalisation decision |

**Calibration anchors (0.00 / 0.33 / 0.67 / 1.00):**

*Transparency sub-dimension:*
- 0.00: No disclosure of logic or decision basis
- 0.33: Limited/technical disclosure; inaccessible to non-experts
- 0.67: Partial explainability with procedural documentation
- 1.00: Full documentation + stakeholder-accessible explainability

*Accountability sub-dimension:*
- 0.00: No assigned responsibility or redress mechanisms
- 0.33: Vague/implicit mandates; weak appeals
- 0.67: Specific mandates + informal grievance procedures
- 1.00: Formal legal accountability, traceability, institutional appeals

*Fairness sub-dimension:*
- 0.00: Evidence of systematic bias; no anti-discrimination safeguard
- 0.33: Ad hoc measures against bias
- 0.67: Bias mitigation without independent validation
- 1.00: Independent bias audits + inclusive design + grievance integration

*Privacy sub-dimension:*
- 0.00: Unfettered data access and sharing
- 0.33: Minimal data minimisation; no consent protocols
- 0.67: Consent-based collection and limited safeguards
- 1.00: Strong encryption + user control + data minimisation + portability

## Corpus Type Clarification

**These 20 cases are national AI governance STRATEGIES/FRAMEWORKS** — cross-jurisdictional comparison of governance configurations for the fsQCA chapter.

**V12_SOM.docx** (uploaded 2026-06-12) = P1 SLR supplementary material. Contains F1–F49 framework list + ADICO coding of those frameworks at the typology level. NOT the fsQCA calibration corpus.

**The two analytical layers:**
- P1 SLR corpus (V12_SOM): 49 frameworks → typology (9 archetypes × 3 dimensions)
- fsQCA chapter corpus (fsqca_SOM_V2): 10 of 20 national strategies → causal configurations
