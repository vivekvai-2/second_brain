# DPB_1 — Establishment, Appointment Process and Continuing Vacancy (2025–2026)

**Institution:** [[DPB]] | **Framework:** [[DPDP_Act_2023]] | **Added:** 2026-08-02 (missing-institutions sourcing pass)

Consolidated node — establishment, procedure, appointment process and the confirmed vacancy, per the PRIS evidence-reuse threshold.

---

## Document Identity

**(a) Constitutive provisions** — DPDP Act 2023 s.18 (establishment), s.19(3) (composition, incl. mandatory legal-expert Member); DPDP Rules 2025 Rules 17–21 (constitution, procedure, terms of service) + Fifth Schedule (terms). Rules 17–21 in force **13 November 2025**, the gazette date — the only part of the Rules in immediate effect.

**(b) MeitY appointment notification** — F. No. 2(1)/2026-Pers.I, Personnel-I Section, **6 May 2026**
https://www.meity.gov.in/static/uploads/2026/05/cd481c027470b420b4cb85fb40a91c53.pdf
Contains the establishment language verbatim ("a body corporate with the mandate to function as an independent adjudicatory authority… digital-by-design") and full applications proforma. 1 Chairperson + 4 Member posts. Chairperson: post equivalent to Additional Secretary to GoI or above; min. age 55; tenure ≤2 years or to age 65; ₹4,50,000/month consolidated (Members ₹4,00,000), no house or car; one-year post-office employment restriction.

**(c) Continuation notification** — 6 June 2026, continuing the appointment process.

**(d) Status assessment** — LiveLaw, 31 July 2026
https://www.livelaw.in/articles/india-data-protection-board-established-law-543751
"As of this month, the Board has no appointed Chairperson and no appointed Members. It exists, in the strict sense, only as a statutory possibility." Search-cum-Selection Committees (Cabinet Secretary chairs for Chairperson; MeitY Secretary for Members, per Rules 17–21) "appear to remain at the stage of soliciting names rather than finalising them."

**(e) Ministerial statement** — IT Secretary S. Krishnan, PTI interview reported 28 December 2025: "the requisite software for a digital-first office of the body has already been developed"; Board operational "in the coming months," no timeline given.

## Confirmed Absences

- **No orders, directions or substantive adjudicatory statements have been issued.** Recorded as a confirmed absence, not inferred from silence — there are no office-holders capable of issuing them.
- **No dedicated official DPBI website located.** Backend software confirmed built; public launch not confirmed. A DSCI-hosted FAQ on the Board's structure exists but is a **third-party document, not a Board publication** — do not cite as official.

## Analytical Significance

**The Rule 4 dependency.** DPDP Rules 2025 **Rule 4 (Consent Manager registration) commences 13 November 2026** and registration is with the Board. An unstaffed Board cannot register. This is the mechanism by which the "enacted but not operative" status recorded at [[06_Frameworks/DEPA]] and [[07_Institutions/Corpus/MeitY/MeitY_4_DPDP_Rules_2025]] may persist past the Rule's own commencement date — not drafting delay but a missing registrar. The same dependency governs every substantive obligation commencing 13 May 2027, all Board-enforced.

**A distinct sequencing pattern — the institutional shell.** The corpus records four "institution before regulation" instances (CSIRT-Power; AA Master Directions preceding DEPA; Sahamati preceding SRO recognition; BIS adopting IS/ISO/IEC 42001 with no accreditation scheme). This is the inverse and analytically worse: statute passed, rules notified, body constituted, software built, **chairs empty**. The others produced capacity ahead of formal authority; this produces formal authority with no capacity. Recommend registering as a named sub-pattern of [[Governance_Debt]].

**Two-layer contestability gap.** [[Contestability_Redress]] records that DPDP creates no ADM right at all. This adds a second layer: the rights DPDP *does* create have no forum. A data principal currently holds statutory rights and a notified complaints procedure, and there is no one to complain to.

**A rare pre-decisional legitimacy case.** [[Legitimacy]] flags the absence of any legitimacy-perception study of an Indian AI or DPI regulator. The DPB can be studied before it has decided anything — an unusual and time-limited research opportunity that closes the moment appointments are made.

## Coding Notes (IG 2.0)

Rules 17–21 and the Fifth Schedule are **constitutive rules** (establishing an institution and its procedure) rather than regulatory rules governing conduct — the same rare category flagged at [[07_Institutions/Corpus/International/International_37_EDPB_AI_Opinions_and_Competence_2024_2026]] for the EU's competence-allocating provisions. India does have constitutive rules; what it lacks is **competence-allocating** rules distributing supervisory jurisdiction between regulators. Worth keeping the two categories distinct in the codebook.
