## Substantive Content

### Cross-sectoral general baseline
Covers data storage device authorisation, backup policy, network monitoring, and content filtering — a general information-security baseline applicable to all designated Critical Sectors, not tailored to power/energy or to AI/ML systems.

### Confirmed absence
No NCIIPC document — in this Guidelines v2.0, the Forty Critical Controls companion, or the broader catalogue of titles reviewed — addresses AI-specific risk categories (model integrity, adversarial ML, algorithmic decision accountability) in any sector, power or otherwise. NCIIPC's role in the power-sector architecture is as the **reporting destination** for CII assets identified under [[07_Institutions/Corpus/CEA/CEA_1_Cyber_Security_Guidelines_2021]], not as an independent source of AI-relevant rules.

---

## Analytical Significance for PRIS Research

### Two-tier structure with no AI layer at either tier
CEA (power-specific, cybersecurity-only) and NCIIPC (cross-sectoral, CII-baseline-only) together form India's power-sector critical-infrastructure cyber-governance architecture. Neither tier addresses AI-specific risk — confirming that the gap identified in this domain is structural (absent from the *entire* architecture) rather than a matter of looking in the wrong single instrument.
