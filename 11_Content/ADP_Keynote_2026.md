## Engagement Summary

This keynote was delivered to ADP's India-based engineering leadership cohort at their annual kickstart meeting. The audience comprised middle and senior managers responsible for engineering teams building payroll, HR, tax, and benefits software. The talk addressed the transition from managing engineers to managing engineers and AI together — positioning the shift not as a threat but as a leadership capability question. The governance section (Sections 4 and 5) drew directly on the PRIS research programme's AI governance and DPDP frameworks, making this the first public deployment of PRIS-derived practitioner content.

## Reusable Frameworks Introduced

### Green / Amber / Red (S18)

A three-bucket AI risk classification tool for enterprise engineering leaders:

- **Green** — Low-risk assistance (drafts, summaries, non-critical analytics). Use freely.
- **Amber** — Medium-stakes workflows or decisions. Human reviews *before* the AI acts, not after.
- **Red** — Core decisions (payroll, compliance, employment outcomes). Human-led; AI is an input, never the author.

**Vault reuse:** [[BFSI_AI_Governance_Handbook]] Chapter 6 (risk classification); [[AI_Governance_Wiki]] practitioner tools section; LinkedIn carousel.

### Five Ps (S19)

A pocket governance checklist for any AI use case:

- **Purpose** — Is the use justified?
- **People** — Who is affected?
- **Proof** — Can we explain it?
- **Process** — Where is human review mandatory?
- **Partner** — Who owns it when it fails?

**Vault reuse:** [[DPDP_Playbook]] Chapter 8 (accountability); [[BFSI_AI_Governance_Handbook]] Chapter 5; consulting diagnostic.

### Four Governance Questions (S17)

For anything AI touches: Can you explain it? Is it actually true? Can you monitor it once live? Who gets the call when it fails?

**Vault reuse:** [[AI_Governance_Wiki]]; executive education slide decks.

### DPDP One-Liner (S20)

*"GDPR taught you rights. DPDP teaches you accountability."*

Three Ps for DPDP: **People** (employee and candidate data is in scope), **Purpose** (data collected for one reason cannot be quietly reused to train a model), **Place** (where data is processed is a design decision made at build time, not a footnote).

**Vault reuse:** [[DPDP_Playbook]] opener; [[Privacy_DataProtection]] domain note; LinkedIn hook.
