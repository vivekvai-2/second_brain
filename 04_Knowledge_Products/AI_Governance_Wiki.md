# **AI Governance Wiki**

**Type:** Knowledge Product  
**Status:** Active — v2.0 (April 2026)  
**Last Updated:** 2026-06-12  
**Audience:** BFSI CISOs, CROs, GCs, DPOs, policymakers, regulators

---

## **Product Description**

The AI Governance Wiki is a living reference resource designed to provide a structured synthesis of Indian and international AI governance frameworks for practitioners, policymakers, regulators, and governance professionals. It functions as the authoritative reference layer within PRIS, consolidating regulatory developments, governance frameworks, implementation guidance, and comparative analysis into a continuously updated knowledge asset. Version 2.0 incorporates corrections to earlier synthesis errors, expanded practitioner commentary, and enhanced coverage of Indian regulatory developments. The current corpus consists of 95 classified PDFs across five intake batches, of which 90 remain in active scope.

## **Coverage Assessment**

The current wiki demonstrates strong coverage across foundational governance concepts, Indian regulatory developments, privacy and security governance, and comparative international frameworks. The strongest sections are India-specific governance content and privacy governance, reflecting sustained corpus development and regulatory monitoring. Coverage weakens where empirical implementation material, incident response practice, sector-specific governance playbooks, and emerging governance domains require additional primary sources. The principal challenge for future development is not breadth but depth in operational governance artefacts, post-incident recovery patterns, and sector-specific implementation guidance.

## **Update Roadmap**

Version 3.0 is targeted for Q3 2026 and will focus on incorporating state-level AI governance initiatives together with an additional intake of approximately 8–12 Tier 1 and Tier 2 source documents. Particular emphasis will be placed on state policy experimentation, healthcare governance, telecom governance, and board-level governance instrumentation.

Version 4.0 is targeted for Q4 2026 following P2 submission and will prioritise developments in agentic AI governance, post-incident governance recovery frameworks, and emerging operational governance practices. Future updates are triggered whenever a material regulatory instrument, guidance document, enforcement action, or governance framework is issued by RBI, SEBI, the Data Protection Board, MeitY, or other major governance institutions.

## **Strategic Value**

The AI Governance Wiki serves as the foundational knowledge asset underpinning the entire PRIS research, consulting, teaching, and thought-leadership ecosystem. Unlike individual papers, its value compounds through continuous updates, cross-domain synthesis, and regulatory accumulation. The product functions as the authoritative source feeding downstream knowledge products, practitioner guidance, executive education, advisory frameworks, and public-facing content. As the corpus expands and governance frameworks mature, the wiki increasingly becomes both a strategic intelligence repository and the primary platform through which research outputs are translated into practical governance value.
