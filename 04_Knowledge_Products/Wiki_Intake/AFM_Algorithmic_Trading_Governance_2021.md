## 3–5 Reusable Findings

1. **Two-phase, two-sector supervisory project design** (feeds S6): The AFM's "2020 Algo-project" examined algorithmic trading governance separately for (a) investment firms (Phase I and Phase II) and (b) trading venues (Phase I and Phase II) — a replicable two-axis (firm-type × phase) supervisory review design for assessing AI/algo governance maturity across market participants.

2. **MAR Article 13 as the governance anchor** (feeds S6): The report frames algorithmic-trading governance requirements around Article 13 of the Market Abuse Regulation (MAR), linking organisational governance obligations directly to market-conduct/abuse-prevention duties — relevant precedent for arguing AI governance obligations should be anchored to existing market-conduct regimes rather than standalone AI rules.

3. **IT security and access controls under Article 18** (feeds S6): Findings on "IT security – Article 18" cover general control weaknesses around system access limits for algorithmic systems — directly transferable checklist item for AI-model access-control audits in BFSI ([[Agentic_AI_Governance]]).
