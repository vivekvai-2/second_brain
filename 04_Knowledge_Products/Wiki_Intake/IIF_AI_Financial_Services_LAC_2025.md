## Summary

The IIF (Institute of International Finance — global banking industry association) provides a regional survey of AI adoption and governance in Latin America and Caribbean (LAC) financial services. The paper documents current-state AI deployment across banking, insurance, and payments in the region, identifies regulatory trajectories across specific LAC jurisdictions (⚠VERIFY country coverage), and characterises the direction of travel over the coming 3–5 years. It is the corpus's first Global South comparator paper focused on LAC — complementing the India-centric focus of the P3 research programme with a genuinely comparable emerging-market region facing analogous polycentric-regulator + capacity-constraint challenges.

## Key Findings and Framework

### LAC AI-in-Finance Adoption Themes

The staff paper documents (⚠VERIFY specific percentages / country data):
- Rapid AI adoption across LAC banking, driven by fraud detection, credit scoring, customer service
- Regulatory heterogeneity across LAC — different countries at different stages of DPA (data protection act) implementation
- Cross-border data-flow tensions between LAC data localisation demands and cloud-hyperscaler business models
- Capacity constraints on LAC regulators mirror those documented in Indian context ([[Governance_Capacity]] concept)
- Emergence of regional-coordination proposals (via IDB LAB, IIF regional working groups)

### Comparative Framing

LAC is characterised as a region where:
1. AI adoption is advancing faster than regulatory capacity to keep pace
2. Regulatory approaches range from principles-based (Brazil LGPD trajectory) to prescriptive (Colombia, Argentina)
3. Multi-jurisdictional operations across LAC produce compliance-cost fragmentation
4. Digital public infrastructure adoption (Sistema PIX in Brazil, Mexico's SPEI, etc.) creates DPI-adjacent AI use cases

### Direction of Travel

The paper projects three to five years of increasing regulatory activity but continued fragmentation; LAC lacks a whole-region coordination mechanism analogous to EU AI Act or ASEAN AI guidelines.
