## 3–5 Reusable Findings

1. **Three sub-narratives of Indian digital sovereignty** (feeds S2): The chapter decomposes India's digital sovereignty discourse into three distinct sub-narratives — economic ("India's data for India's development"), security ("digital strikes"), and democracy ("data as empowerment") — each legitimising different, sometimes conflicting, policy interventions.

2. **Sovereignty as narrative, not fixed policy doctrine** (feeds S2): The chapter's central conceptual claim is that "digital sovereignty" functions as a flexible legitimising narrative rather than a rigid policy objective — a caution against treating it as a stable analytic category when comparing India to Western sovereignty frameworks.

3. **Narrative-to-policy-outcome mapping** (feeds S2): The chapter traces the narrative directly into concrete policy outcomes — cross-border data flow restrictions, platform accountability rules, critical-technology production autonomy, and DPI (India Stack) expansion — providing a reusable narrative-to-instrument mapping template.

4. **Caution against imported geopolitical lenses** (feeds S2/S3): The chapter explicitly warns that applying a Western geopolitical lens to India's (and other emerging economies') digital governance strategy risks inaccurate conclusions, given sovereignty's use there as a contestable, adaptable narrative rather than a settled legal principle.
