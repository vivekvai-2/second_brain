## Key Findings and Framework

### Mixed-Method Design (SEM + fsQCA) — the methodologically distinctive contribution

Unlike F086 (pure QCA) and F087/F088 (pure fsQCA), this paper runs **both** a PLS-SEM structural model **and** an fsQCA on the same 225-respondent dataset, explicitly treating them as complementary: SEM identifies net-effects predictors (trust, affect, privacy concern); fsQCA identifies which *configurations* of the same antecedent conditions are jointly sufficient for high behavioral intention (hBI) to use OGD websites. This "SEM-then-fsQCA" sequencing on identical survey data is a directly reusable design template if PRIS ever wants to pair a variance-based test with a configurational one on the same case set.

### Definitions

- **Open government data (OGD):** data not subject to copyright/patent/regulatory restriction, reusable and distributable by anyone commercially or non-commercially.
- **Trust (in this context):** willingness to rely on an unfamiliar trustee absent credible information or emotional connection.
- **Privacy concern:** individuals' views about what will happen to information they give to different providers.
- **Social cognitive theory (SCT):** human activity determined by the interaction of individual behavior, individual cognition, and environment.
- Relationship-marketing bonds taxonomy: **economic bond** (price/incentive-based), **social bond** (relationship/emotional), **structural bond** (value-added service-based) — all significant SEM predictors of trust (β = 0.148, 0.266, 0.234 respectively).

### Named Incident (privacy-relevant, not AI-specific)

**2020 Wuhan epidemic data leak:** cited as a real privacy-breach example — personal information of returnees from Wuhan was leaked during epidemic data opening/sharing. A useful India-comparator data point (non-AI) for any future PRIS discussion of public-health data governance failures, alongside the corpus's existing AI-era privacy-breach material.
