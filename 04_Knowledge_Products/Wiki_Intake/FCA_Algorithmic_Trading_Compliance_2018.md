## 3–5 Reusable Findings

1. **Four-pillar review structure** (feeds S6): The FCA structures its multi-firm review of algorithmic trading compliance around four pillars — governance and oversight, development and testing, risk controls, and market conduct — offering a four-part diagnostic template directly reusable for AI-system governance reviews ahead of binding rules (analogous to the pre-RBI-FREE-AI advisory period in India).

2. **Pre-implementation regulatory stocktake methodology** (feeds S3): Published ahead of MiFID II implementation, the report functions as a "readiness stocktake" of firm practice against forthcoming binding rules — methodologically comparable to assessing Indian BFSI readiness ahead of any RBI FREE-AI recommendations converting from advisory ("should") to binding ("shall") status. ⚠ VERIFY — link to RBI_FREE_AI_2025 open VERIFY flag #5 (should→shall conversion) should be made explicit in indexing.

3. **Governance and oversight as the foundational pillar** (feeds S6): The FCA places "governance and oversight" first among its four review pillars, ahead of technical development/testing controls — supporting an argument that governance-layer maturity (board sign-off, accountability lines) is a prerequisite for, not a consequence of, technical AI-control maturity.
