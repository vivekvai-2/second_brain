## 3–5 Reusable Findings

1. **1,200-source synthesis into a single lifecycle framework** (feeds S6): CSET collates guidance from over 1,200 academic papers, industry reports, and grey-literature sources into a single "harmonized AI framework" spanning the full AI adoption lifecycle from planning through improvement — addressing the gap between high-level AI principles and granular implementation detail.

2. **Sixteen-stage operationalisation sequence** (feeds S6): The guide's implementation sequence — Self-Assess, Define, Plan, Promote, Map, Assess, Acquire, Develop, Control, Protect, Test, Approve, Communicate, Deploy, Engage, Monitor, Respond, Improve — offers a directly reusable maturity-stage taxonomy for enterprise AI governance program design.

3. **What/When/Where/Who/How mapping across each step** (feeds S6): Each implementation step is mapped across technique, lifecycle stage, organisational level, responsible function, and supporting references — closing the "principles vs. practice" gap the authors identify as the primary failure mode in organisational AI adoption.
