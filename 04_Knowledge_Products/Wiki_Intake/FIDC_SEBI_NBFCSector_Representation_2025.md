## 3–5 Reusable Findings

1. **HVDLE Threshold Rationalisation Request** (feeds S2): FIDC requests SEBI to increase the "High Value Debt Listed Entity" (HVDLE) threshold from ₹100 crore to ₹500 crore ⚠ VERIFY BEFORE PUBLISHING: exact current threshold and proposed threshold — to rationalise which NBFCs fall under SEBI's LODR (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) disclosure regime for listed debt. NBFCs below the revised threshold argue the compliance burden is disproportionate relative to investor protection benefit.

2. **Cross-Regulatory Cybersecurity Standards Fragmentation** (feeds S2/S6): FIDC's submission explicitly raises the issue of **fragmented cybersecurity standards** — SEBI CSCRF, RBI cyber security framework, and CERT-In directions impose overlapping, non-harmonised cyber obligations on dual-regulated NBFCs. FIDC requests a joint working group of SEBI, RBI, and CERT-In to develop unified cybersecurity standards for regulated entities. This is direct practitioner evidence of [[05_Concepts/05_Concepts_Signature/Regulatory_Parallelism]] in BFSI cyber governance — sector bodies experiencing and formally objecting to multi-regulator cyber fragmentation.

3. **Sub-Debt and Perpetual Debt Instrument (PDI) Issuance Norms** (feeds S2): FIDC requests SEBI to align sub-debt/PDI listing norms for NBFCs with those applicable to banks, citing asymmetry in capital-raising ability between bank-issued and NBFC-issued subordinated instruments under current SEBI LODR and ICDR frameworks. Relevant to the regulatory-gap analysis in P4 (doctrinal BFSI parity).

4. **KYC Harmonisation — Unified KYC for BFSI** (feeds S2/S6): FIDC requests a single, portable KYC record recognised by all SEBI-regulated and RBI-regulated entities, eliminating duplicative KYC processes for customers transacting with both. Current fragmentation imposes compliance cost on NBFCs and friction for customers. Consistent with the regulatory parallelism pattern documented across the corpus.

5. **ISIN Limit Rationalisation for Listed Debt** (feeds S2): FIDC requests SEBI to increase per-issuer ISIN limits for listed NBFC debt from 9 to 21 ISINs per maturity bucket, citing operational constraints in debt programme management for large NBFC issuers. This is a technical market-access issue with limited AI governance relevance.
