## 3–5 Reusable Findings

1. **Enterprise adoption scale** (feeds S1): OpenAI reports over 1 million business customers; ChatGPT message volume grew 8× year-over-year and API reasoning-token consumption per organisation grew 320× year-over-year — headline adoption-velocity figures from the market-leading vendor.

2. **Self-reported productivity claim** (feeds S1): Enterprise users report saving 40–60 minutes per day — an OpenAI-asserted figure with no independent methodology disclosed; treat as vendor-claimed, not independently verified, consistent with the vault's standard caveat for self-reported vendor productivity statistics (cf. F074 McKinsey $4.4T figure).

3. **Sectoral adoption differential** (feeds S1): Median sector usage grew more than 6× over the past 12 months, with the technology sector leading at 11× — evidence of uneven adoption velocity across industry sectors.

4. **Within-enterprise adoption inequality ("frontier" gap)** (feeds S1): "Frontier" workers send 6× more messages than the median user, and "frontier" firms send 2× as many messages per seat as the median enterprise — a bimodal adoption-intensity signal relevant to [[05_Concepts/05_Concepts_Standard/Governance_Capacity]]: fast-adopting units within an enterprise are likely outpacing governance maturity, a plausible Governance_Debt precursor not directly measured in this excerpt.
