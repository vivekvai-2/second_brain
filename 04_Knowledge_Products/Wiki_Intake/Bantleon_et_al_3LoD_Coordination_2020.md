## 3–5 Reusable Findings

1. **Large-sample CAE survey base** (feeds S6): The study analyses survey responses from 415 Chief Audit Executives (CAEs) across Austria, Germany, and Switzerland, funded by the DIIR (Institut für Interne Revision) — providing one of the largest empirical bases in this batch's 3LoD cluster and a methodological precedent (large CAE survey) for any future PRIS survey-based fsQCA data collection on AI governance coordination.

2. **3LoD framed as a coordination/communication tool, not just a control structure** (feeds S6): The paper frames the TLoD model's core purpose as improving "coordination and enhance[d] communications on risk management and control" by clarifying roles/duties of governance functions, explicitly warning that without effective coordination "work can be duplicated" — directly applicable to arguments that poorly coordinated AI governance functions (e.g., overlapping model-risk and AI-ethics committees) replicate this duplication risk.

3. **Variance in coordination challenges by determinant and stakeholder** (feeds S6): The study finds "great variance in the extent of coordination challenges" depending on different determinants and the specific governance stakeholder (internal audit vs. other lines) — supports a configurational (fsQCA-style) rather than uniform analytical approach to AI governance coordination, reinforcing methodological alignment with [[fsQCA_Thesis_Chapter]].
