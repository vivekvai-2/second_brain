## Key Findings and Framework

### Sub-symbolic vs. symbolic AI — a clean explainability-trade-off anchor

"Sub-symbolic AI (deep learning)" is characterised as an "opaque data-based system," while "symbolic AI offers inspectability, interpretability, and explainability" (Calegari, Ciatto & Omicini, 2020). A concise, citable two-category explainability-trade-off framing, complementary to (but simpler than) [[04_Knowledge_Products/Wiki_Intake/F085_Sudjianto_Zhang_Model_Validation_Practice_Banking_2024]]'s granular SHAP/LIME/PDP/ALE explainability-methods taxonomy — useful as a plain-language framing device for practitioner-facing content on the transparency/performance trade-off in AI system design.

### Five levels of sentiment analysis (Feldman, 2013)

Document-level, sentence-level, aspect-level, comparative sentiment analysis, sentiment-lexicon acquisition — a technical granularity taxonomy for any future PRIS work analysing citizen sentiment toward Indian government digital services (e.g., social-media sentiment on UPI, Aadhaar, or DBT schemes).

### Bibliometric findings — publication growth and thematic structure

353 articles (2010–2021) analysed; publication volume grew from 2 articles (2010) to 95 (2021) — steep growth curve. Four thematic clusters via co-citation analysis: social intelligence for civic administration; sentiment analysis for citizen engagement; sentiment-analysis techniques; sentiment analysis for smart cities. *Government Information Quarterly* itself is the most-cited source journal in the field (163 total citations across 2 papers in the reviewed sample) — a data point reinforcing GIQ's centrality to the broader e-government/digital-governance literature this corpus already draws on heavily (F086, F089, F091, F092, F105, F107–F114).

### Governance-adjacent recommendations (light touch)

The paper's own conclusions include governance-relevant advisory language: "Citizens should be educated and sensitized on data usage and data privacy policies," and calls for "encryption algorithms for enhanced data security" — general data-protection-hygiene recommendations, not substantive AI-governance analysis, but consistent with the corpus's broader data-privacy thread (DPDP Rules, MeitY_4).
