## 3–5 Reusable Findings

1. **Sectoral RAI maturity gradient** (feeds S2): A survey of 535 senior executives found ~38% of BFSI firms, ~30% of TMT firms, and ~21% of healthcare firms report "matured" responsible-AI (RAI) practices and policies, with roughly a further third in each sector having initiated formal RAI steps — establishing BFSI as the relative India RAI maturity leader ahead of MeitY/RBI binding instruments.

2. **Workforce investment as maturity driver** (feeds S7): 89% of organisations reporting matured RAI practices committed to continued investment in workforce sensitisation/training, versus 60%+ of lower-maturity organisations making the same commitment — positions workforce capability-building as a leading indicator of RAI maturity, useful for practitioner capacity-building narratives.

3. **Confidence-compliance inversion** (feeds S2): ~49% of respondents reported highest confidence in meeting data-protection obligations, while ~59% reported lowest confidence specifically in meeting regulatory compliance obligations (versus transparency/explainability) — evidences a gap between data-handling competence and regulatory-readiness even among RAI-mature firms, ⚠ VERIFY exact wording/denominator before citing externally.

4. **India BFSI as early-mover baseline (2023)** (feeds S2): The ~38% BFSI maturity figure (pre-dating RBI FREE-AI, Nov 2025) establishes an industry self-reported baseline against which the FREE-AI advisory framework's actual uptake can later be benchmarked — useful anchor point for [[Regulatory_Parallelism]] timeline work.
