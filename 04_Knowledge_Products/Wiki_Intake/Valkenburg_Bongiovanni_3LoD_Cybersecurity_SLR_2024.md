## Summary

Valkenburg & Bongiovanni conduct a systematic literature review of the Three Lines of Defence (3LoD) model as applied to cybersecurity governance. The paper documents that 3LoD — originating in the 2013 IIA paper and dominant in enterprise risk management post-GFC — has been applied to cybersecurity in a fragmented and inconsistent manner across the academic and practitioner literature. Using grounded theory methodology on the SLR corpus, the authors identify variation in how first-, second-, and third-line responsibilities are allocated for cybersecurity risk, and where the 3LoD model succeeds or fails in the cybersecurity domain specifically. The paper is the direct 2024 systematic-review companion to F007–F010 (Schuett 2023, Davies 2018, Arndorfer/Minto 2015, Bantleon et al 2020) covering the cybersecurity-specific dimension of 3LoD scholarship.

## Key Findings

### 3LoD Application to Cybersecurity — SLR Findings

| Dimension | 3LoD Original (IIA 2013) | Cybersecurity Application Reality |
|---|---|---|
| **First line** | Business operations owning risk | CISO teams; DevSecOps; often disputed whether first or second line |
| **Second line** | Risk / Compliance oversight | InfoSec function; frequently overlaps with first line in flat organisations |
| **Third line** | Internal Audit (independence) | Cyber audit function; often understaffed or externalised |
| **Coordination** | Board-level integration | Frequently absent in cybersecurity domain despite Board-level cyber risk visibility |

### Central Findings (Grounded Theory)

- 3LoD in cybersecurity is *institutionally inconsistent*: same term used for different structural configurations across firms and jurisdictions
- Cybersecurity's rapid-response demands strain the 3LoD's slower audit-cycle architecture
- Post-2020 renaming to "Three Lines Model" (removing "Defence") is superficial; underlying structural ambiguity remains
- Enterprise risk management frameworks post-GFC generalised 3LoD without adequate domain-specific adaptation for cybersecurity

### SLR Grounded-Theory Categories

The paper's SLR-derived taxonomy captures how 3LoD is:
1. Structurally applied (line-allocation variations)
2. Procedurally applied (rhythm/tempo mismatches)
3. Governance-integrated (Board reporting vs siloed operation)
4. Critically evaluated (empirical failures + normative critiques)
