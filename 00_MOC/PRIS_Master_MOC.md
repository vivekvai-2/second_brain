## **What PRIS Is**

PRIS (Personal Regulatory Intelligence System) is a Research Intelligence Platform designed to convert academic literature, regulatory analysis, coding outputs, consulting insights, and AI-generated analysis into reusable intellectual assets that compound over a twenty-year horizon. It is not a second brain and not a note collection. PRIS exists to support the systematic production of Research, Teaching, Consulting, Thought Leadership, and Knowledge Products through a structured knowledge architecture linking strategy, projects, concepts, frameworks, and outputs.

## **System Boundaries**

|**System**|**Authoritative For**|**Never Replicate**|
|---|---|---|
|Zotero|Literature corpus and references|Full bibliographies|
|NVivo|IG 2.0 coding and ADICO nodes|Coding trees and node structures|
|iCloud PDFs / Policy Dump|Regulatory documents and source materials|Document content and full-text extracts|
|Obsidian — Corpus Nodes (07_Institutions/Corpus/)|Derived intelligence notes on regulatory instruments (Format A)|Primary-source full text — notes are synthesis + connections only|
|Obsidian — Wiki Intake (04_Knowledge_Products/Wiki_Intake/)|Derived intelligence notes on academic/Big4/advisory reports (Format B)|Primary-source full text — notes are synthesis + connections only|

> Clarification (v2.0): the "never replicate" rule prohibits storing primary-source text in Obsidian. It does not prohibit derived intelligence notes *about* those sources — see [[PRIS_Master_Ontology_v2]] §1.
