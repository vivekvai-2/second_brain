## 6. Kallinikos, J., Aaltonen, A., & Marton, A. (2013)

**Full citation:** Kallinikos, J., Aaltonen, A., & Marton, A. (2013). The ambivalent ontology of digital artefacts. *MIS Quarterly*, *37*(2), 357–370.  
**Zotero key:** `kallinikos_aaltonen_marton_2013`  
**Role:** Theoretical anchor — primary  
**Claim:** Digital artefacts possess four properties — editability, interactivity, openness, and distributedness — that distinguish them from physical objects and determine how they distribute agency, authority, and accountability across sociotechnical systems.  
**Where used:** §2 (establishes the data-as-records theoretical lineage); §5 Beat 3 (the rule architecture is analysed as possessing these four properties — versions, authorities, portals, technical coupling map to editability, interactivity, openness, distributedness)
