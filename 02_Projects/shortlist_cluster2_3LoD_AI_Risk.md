## 1. Landscape Map

The Three Lines of Defence (3LoD) literature divides into three camps: **3LoD-as-adaptable**, **3LoD-as-insufficient**, and **hybrid/redesign proposals**. Key waypoints: the 2020 IIA "Three Lines Model" update; the BIS FSI four-lines-of-defence model adding an external-supervisor line (Arndorfer & Minto, 2015); Eulerich's (2021) reformulation of the updated IIA model; the Unified Control Framework (arXiv, 2025) integrating NIST AI RMF, ISO 23894, and the EU AI Act; and SR 11-7 successors. Named contributors: Schuett (2023), Luburić (2017), Davies & Zhivitskaya (2018), Butler (2024), Bantleon et al. (2021), Kurshan et al. (2020), Danielsson et al. (2022), Addae et al. (2023), McGee (2024).

**Direct quote (critical gap):** "No single systematic review or meta-analysis of 3LoD across AI conditions exists."
