# Butler, Gozman & Lyytinen (2023)

**Full citation:** Butler, T., Gozman, D., & Lyytinen, K. (2023). Regulating artificial intelligence: How the EU AI Act will shape the governance of AI systems in financial services. *Journal of Information Technology*, *38*(4), 378–398.  
**Zotero key:** `butler_gozman_lyytinen_2023`  
**Journal:** Journal of Information Technology (ABDC-A)

---

## Core Argument

Regulatory frameworks for AI systems are constructible as IS objects — their architecture, grammar, and structural properties are amenable to information systems analysis, not merely legal or policy analysis. The paper demonstrates this through analysis of the EU AI Act's governance architecture for financial services, showing how regulatory text encodes obligations that reshape enterprise information management in ways that exceed simple compliance cost.

---

## Why This Work Matters to PRIS

This paper is the foundational warrant for treating regulatory instruments as IS objects. Without Butler et al., the claim that IG 2.0 coding of governance documents is an IS contribution rather than a legal one is unsupported. It licenses the analytical move that P2 and P3 both make: analysing the structure of governance instruments as an IS research object, not just reading them as legal text.
