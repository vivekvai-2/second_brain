# Seidel, Frick & vom Brocke (2025)

**Full citation:** Seidel, S., Frick, C. J., & vom Brocke, J. (2025). Regulating emerging technologies: Prospective sensemaking through abstraction and elaboration. *MIS Quarterly*, *49*(1), 179–204.  
**Zotero key:** `seidel_frick_vombrocke_2025`  
**Journal:** MIS Quarterly (AIS Senior Scholars' Basket — ABDC-A*)

---

## Core Argument

Regulatory frameworks for emerging technologies are constructed prospectively — before the technology is fully understood — through two moves: abstraction (establishing high-level principles that survive technical uncertainty) and elaboration (translating principles into operational obligations as the technology matures). The paper theorises how regulators make sense of technologies they cannot yet fully specify.

---

## Why This Work Matters to PRIS

This is the primary **complementary positioning anchor** for both P2 and P3. The paper asks: how are regulatory frameworks for emerging technologies *built*? P2 asks: what structural properties does the rule architecture of DPI governance instruments *contain* once built? P3 asks: what structural *absences* do AI governance frameworks contain once assembled, and what do those absences *cost* the enterprise? The "complementary" framing is precise — different questions, same domain, mutually reinforcing contributions.

**Critical instruction:** Both P2 and P3 must use the word "complementary" explicitly when citing this paper. It is not a contrasting position. It is not a gap the papers fill. It is a related but distinct contribution that P2/P3 extend.
