# Frantz & Siddiki (2022)

**Full citation:** Frantz, C., & Siddiki, S. (2022). *Institutional grammar: Foundations and applications for institutional analysis*. Palgrave Macmillan.  
**Zotero key:** `frantz_siddiki_2022`  
**Publisher:** Palgrave Macmillan  
**Year:** 2022 — **not 2021**. Confirmed in P2 scaffold v3.

---

## Core Argument

Institutional Grammar 2.0 refines the original Crawford-Ostrom ADICO grammar with three improvements: (1) explicit classification of institutional statements as Rules (ADICO-complete with Or-else), Norms (ADIC without Or-else), or Shared Strategies (AIC without Deontic or Or-else); (2) updated coding protocols that improve inter-rater reliability; (3) application examples across governance domains enabling cross-study comparability.

---

## Why This Work Matters to PRIS

Frantz & Siddiki (2022) is the **working specification** for all IG 2.0 coding in the PRIS research programme. While Crawford & Ostrom (1995) provides the intellectual lineage, Frantz & Siddiki provides the operational protocols that distinguish Rules from Norms from Shared Strategies — the distinction that makes the P2 finding ("18 Rules, 0 rights-bearing") and the P3 finding ("91 AI-relevant requirement units") precise rather than approximate.
