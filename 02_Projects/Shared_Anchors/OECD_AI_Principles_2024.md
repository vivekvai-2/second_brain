# OECD AI Principles (2024)

**Full citation:** OECD. (2024). *Recommendation of the Council on Artificial Intelligence* (OECD/LEGAL/0449). Organisation for Economic Co-operation and Development.  
**Earlier version:** OECD. (2019). *Recommendation of the Council on Artificial Intelligence* (OECD/LEGAL/0449). (Updated 2024 to incorporate generative AI considerations.)  
**Zotero key:** `oecd_ai_principles_2024`

---

## Core Argument

The OECD AI Principles (2019, updated 2024) are the foundational international normative reference for ethical and trustworthy AI, organised around five value-based principles (inclusive growth, rule of law and human rights, transparency and explainability, robustness/security/safety, accountability) and five government recommendations. They are non-binding, operating through political commitment and norm diffusion. The 2024 update incorporates generative AI and foundation model considerations at the principles level without introducing enforcement mechanisms.

---

## Why This Work Matters to PRIS

The OECD AI Principles are the **international normative baseline** for the fsQCA model and the primary reference for understanding how India's IndiaAI Framework relates to the international normative landscape. In P4, they provide a comparative anchor for India's principles-based approach. In P5, they are one of four frameworks whose institutional answerability gap P5's architecture fills.
