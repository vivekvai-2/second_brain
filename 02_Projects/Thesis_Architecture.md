## 2. Integration Logic

The thesis is a **paper-based PhD** (also called publication-based or article-based thesis). The four papers (P2, P3, P4, fsQCA chapter) form the empirical core. The binding integrative chapter (Chapter 9) must:

- Demonstrate theoretical coherence across the four empirical contributions
- Show how Regulatory Parallelism, Governance Debt, and Institutional Coherence constitute a unified research programme rather than four independent studies
- Connect the DPI governance findings (P2) to the BFSI governance findings (P3/P4) through the shared construct of Institutional Coherence
- Situate the fsQCA configurational findings within the broader governance theory literature
