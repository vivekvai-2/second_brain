## 1. Context Pointer

This file tracks the operational state of P3. For the strategic rationale — why JEIM, why this sequence, what P3 contributes to the PhD defence gate — read `VV_Publication_Pipeline_v3.md` **Section 5.2**.

**P3's role in the portfolio:** P3 is the second paper in the publication sequence (P2 → P3 → P4 → P1). It is the **fastest-decision ABDC-A journal in the portfolio** — typical first decision 3–5 months from submission, compared to 4–8 months for R&G. If P2 JSIS does not yield a strong signal by Jan 2027, P3 JEIM becomes the primary defence-gate candidate.

**Critical distinction from P4:** P3 and P4 share the BFSI corpus. Their boundary is absolute and must be maintained throughout execution:

| Paper | Angle | Journal | Theory | Text reuse |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **P3** | Enterprise IS — how structural mandate incoherence creates compliance burden for organisations | JEIM (ABDC-A) | Enterprise Information Management (EIM); ISG as subdomain | Zero with P4 |
| **P4** | Doctrinal/regulatory — legal analysis of multi-regulator AI governance complexity | CLSR (ABDC-B) | Regulatory design theory | Zero with P3 |

> **P3 is not a governance/regulatory paper. It is an enterprise information management paper that uses regulatory architecture as its explanatory variable.** Every editorial decision must maintain this framing. The moment "cybersecurity governance" or "ISG framework" resurfaces as the primary frame in a section heading or paragraph topic sentence, the JEIM framing has drifted. Section 2.1 and the title are the anchors.
