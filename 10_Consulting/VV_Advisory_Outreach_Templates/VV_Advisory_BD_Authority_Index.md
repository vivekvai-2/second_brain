## What This Library Actually Contains

Not all 52 items are communication templates. The library spans eight distinct document types — outreach messages (Bucket A), proposals and applications (most of Bucket B), publication-format scaffolds (Bucket D), content-production systems (Bucket C), trackers and decision worksheets (Bucket E), and entity-specific ready-to-fill instances (Bucket F). Treat the index below by column, not by assuming uniform genre — a downstream agent reading this vault should not assume every note here is a message to send.

## Maintenance Protocol

A commercially mature library is not one with more templates — it is one where usage discipline is enforced. Going forward:

1. **Track actual use.** Log which template was sent, to whom, and with what outcome in [[Partner_Entity_Profile_Card_Template]]'s notes field — without this, tier classification above will drift from reality within a quarter.
2. **Measure conversion**, not completion. A template being fully filled out is not evidence of business development; a response, citation, or meeting is.
3. **Retire weak templates.** If a Conditional or Scaffold template sits unused for two consecutive quarters, mark it Retire/Merge-Candidate and fold it into the nearest overlapping template.
4. **Promote proven language into the Modular Blocks Library**, not into individual templates — when a specific Call-to-Action or opening line demonstrably converts, update [[Modular_Blocks_Library]] directly.
5. **Build entity-specific (Bucket F) instances only near execution**, not speculatively — the two stale deadlines found this session are the direct cost of the opposite habit.
