## Usage Notes

- IDRC explicitly requires institutional affiliation — do not submit as an individual applicant; this is a hard gate, not a preference.
- **Conditional tier:** do not pre-fill or send this until the corresponding opportunity is verified live (correct gate status, correct entity contact, current initiative) — this is a template, not a standing to-do.
