# Modular Blocks Library — VV Advisory Outreach

**Type:** Reusable Prose Block Library
**Status:** Active
**Created:** 2026-07-15
**Purpose:** Bucket A (Partner-Category Outreach) and Bucket B (Assignment-Type) templates in [[VV_Advisory_BD_Authority_Index]] assemble from these six canonical blocks rather than each restating its own credibility paragraph, bio, value proposition, and call-to-action in full prose. This is the fix for the duplication risk flagged across ~25 overlapping templates: one canonical version of each block, edited in one place, rather than 25 independently drifting copies.

**How to use:** Every Bucket A/B template's "Modular Blocks to Assemble" section names which blocks apply. Copy the named block(s) below into the outgoing document, then insert the entity-specific and assignment-specific content the template's own field table calls for. Do not rewrite a block's canonical language inside an individual template file — edit it here, once, and every template that references it stays current.

---

## Block 1 — Partner-Category Opening

One canonical opening paragraph per Bucket A category, establishing why VV is writing to this category of entity at all before narrowing to the specific ask.

| Category | Canonical Opening |
|---|---|
| Central Government & Regulatory Body | "I write as an AI governance practitioner-researcher (IIM Indore FPM candidate, CISSP, 25+ years in Information Security/Governance/IT leadership) working on cross-regulator enforcement logic for AI systems in Indian BFSI, DPI, and GCC contexts." |
| Indian Think Tank | "Your published work on [cite specific piece] operates at the policy-analysis layer; my current research translates that analysis into practitioner-operational frameworks — a complementary rather than competing lens on the same problem." |
| Global Think Tank / International Body | "I bring a India-specific, cross-regulator practitioner perspective (DPDP + CERT-In + RBI + SEBI + IRDAI) that is under-represented in the current global AI governance discourse, alongside an Army/CISSP security background relevant to your risk-management work." |
| Private GRC / Boutique Consulting Firm | "Your firm delivers [ISO 27001/42001/GRC implementation] without an open, published cross-regulator AI governance methodology for Indian BFSI/DPI clients — I am the methodology architect to your delivery machinery." |
| Industry Body | "Your members face overlapping DPDP/CERT-In/RBI/SEBI obligations for AI systems with no unified enforcement logic — I would like to contribute [research/guidance/speaking] addressing this directly." |
| Foundation / Funder | "My work sits at the intersection of your [technology policy / digital economy / governance] programme and India's AI regulatory reality — a Global South case study your portfolio may currently lack." |
| State Government | "Your state's [named live initiative] lacks a cross-regulator enforcement map connecting DPDP, CERT-In, and AI risk controls — I can convert your existing framework into an audit-ready assurance architecture." |

## Block 2 — Assignment-Specific Value Proposition

Not a fixed paragraph — pull the entity's own "Value proposition to crystallise" line verbatim from [[VV_Partner_Map_Master]] (each entity profile has one) and insert it here unedited. This block is deliberately per-entity, not canonical prose, because the value proposition is the one place genuine differentiation belongs — do not template this into sameness.

---

## Block 3 — Evidence / Credential Module

The single canonical bio/credential paragraph, reused verbatim everywhere a bio is needed:

> "Vivek Vaidyanathan is an Executive PhD (FPM) candidate at IIM Indore (Information Systems), CISSP-credentialed, with 25+ years of leadership experience spanning Program Management, Operations, Information Security, Governance, IT/Telecom, and R&D — including senior roles bridging technology governance and operational security. Current research focuses on the intersection of AI governance, e-governance, privacy regulation, and institutional design, with a specific emphasis on cross-regulator enforcement logic for India's BFSI and DPI ecosystems."

Shorten to one sentence for space-constrained contexts (e.g. speaker bios): "IIM Indore FPM candidate, CISSP, 25+ years in Information Security/Governance/IT leadership, researching cross-regulator AI governance enforcement logic for Indian BFSI and DPI."

## Block 4 — India-Governance Positioning Module

The canonical distinctive-claim paragraph, per [[VV_Integrated_Strategy_Master]] Section 1.2:

> "India's AI governance landscape has four occupied quadrants — policy analysis (Esya, Takshashila), framework implementation (Judge India, Coral eSecure), closed-door government advisory (CeRAI), and government-adjacent research (Vidhi, CSTEP) — and one white space: a public-facing institution translating cross-regulator enforcement logic (DPDP, CERT-In, RBI, SEBI, IRDAI, TRAI) into frameworks policymakers cite and enterprises actually deploy. That is the distinctive claim underlying this work."

## Block 5 — Call-to-Action Module

Three variants — select per assignment type, do not default to the same CTA everywhere:

| Variant | Use When | Canonical Text |
|---|---|---|
| Request meeting/call | Outreach to firms, industry bodies, RegTech companies | "I would welcome a brief 15-20 minute call to discuss how this might fit your current priorities." |
| Request citation/publication | Think tank pitches, working paper submissions | "I would be glad to have this considered for [publication venue] — happy to adapt format or length to your editorial requirements." |
| Request slot/appointment | Committee submissions, empanelment, fellowship, board applications | "I have attached the required documentation and would welcome the opportunity to be considered for [specific slot/role/empanelment category]." |

---

## Block 6 — Conflict / Scope Disclaimer Module

Use whenever an outreach could plausibly be read as touching defence-adjacent scope, per the standing rule in [[Inbound_Pipeline_Index]] and [[Advisory_Retainer_SOW_Template]]:

> "This engagement is scoped to [commercial/enterprise/BFSI/civilian-government] AI governance only. I am not able to advise on defence, defence-procurement, or defence-adjacent matters while serving, per standing service-conduct constraints."

Omit this block entirely for outreach with no plausible defence reading (e.g. Esya Centre working paper pitch) — including it where irrelevant reads as boilerplate and undermines the "no fluff" positioning.
