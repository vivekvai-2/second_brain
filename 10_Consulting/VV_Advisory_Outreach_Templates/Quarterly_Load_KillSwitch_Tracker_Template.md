## Usage Notes

- The kill-switch rule is absolute per [[VV_Integrated_Strategy_Master]] Section 9: 'No short-term mandate justifies PhD slippage' — this tracker exists to make that rule enforceable rather than aspirational.
- Q3-Q4 2026 are pre-flagged Overloaded in the source model — expect kill-switch activation in those quarters and do not treat it as failure.
- **Scaffold tier:** structural placeholder. Useful as a starting frame, but not actively maintained against changing facts — verify every field before relying on it, more so than for Core/Instance items.
