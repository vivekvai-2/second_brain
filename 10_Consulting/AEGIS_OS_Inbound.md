## What They Do

AEGIS OS is a sovereign, runtime decision control layer that sits between AI/automation systems and execution — enforcing policy, risk thresholds, and validation checks before any action is committed. It is deployable on-premise and air-gapped, integrates via APIs or sidecar, and fits into non-sensitive or adjacent layers including simulation, training, and internal workflows.

**Core capabilities:**
- Pre-execution enforcement: every action validated in 10–50ms per decision before commitment
- Post-quantum cryptography (PQC)-ready: protects communications and decision logs against harvest-now-decrypt-later threats
- Tamper-proof audit trails: hash-linked logs (blockchain/Merkle-style) ensuring non-repudiation and immutability
- Full traceability: who / what / why for every decision, replayable under audit
- Runtime controls: authority binding (RBAC/ABAC), grounding and admissibility checks, fail-safe kill switch in under 50ms

**Claimed operational impact:** 40–70% reduction in invalid or unsafe executions; sub-50ms control latency; full audit readiness with provable decisions.

**Defence scenarios cited:** Electronic warfare / jamming (DDIL conditions); autonomous system testing (kill switch); logistics under uncertainty (fuel/ammo allocation AI); intelligence workflow validation (confidence + source admissibility checks before dissemination).

**Positioning:** Sovereign governance and safety layer that strengthens the control plane — ensuring decisions are not just intelligent, but provably safe, compliant, and resilient.
