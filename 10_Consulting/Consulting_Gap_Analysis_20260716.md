**Type:** Consulting Intelligence — Vault-Wide Gap Assessment
**Status:** Active
**Scope:** Consultancy track only (execution-ready assets, deliverables, sector coverage, BD pipeline). Academic/theory-track gaps (P1–P4, fsQCA, records-rules asymmetry cluster) explicitly out of scope — flagged elsewhere for later.

## Headline

The vault has a large, well-evidenced corpus (140+ Wiki_Intake nodes, 12 institution tracks) and a genuinely mature consulting-template architecture (10_Consulting/). The gap is not content. It is **three specific translation failures**: corpus growth has outpaced knowledge-product synthesis, sector coverage in the corpus has outpaced sector coverage in deliverables, and a well-designed BD roadmap has zero recorded execution.

## Gap 4 — Diagnostic-methodology gaps within existing templates

[[Governance_Diagnostic_Report_Template]]'s Section 2 (Parallelism Diagnostic) uses a seven-dimension binary checklist. This session's F124 (Viljanen & Parviainen, five-layer AI regulatory stratigraphy) supplies a sharper diagnostic axis — *layer-correctness* (is an instrument operating at the right layer: data / application-specific-AI / general-AI / application-specific-non-AI / general-non-AI) — not yet incorporated into the template. This is a low-effort template upgrade, not new research: the corpus evidence exists (F124, plus [[Institutional_Coherence]]'s F124 Instances-in-Corpus entry), only the template needs the additional column.

Similarly, F123's audit-validity concept ("epistemological-roots transparency" — auditing what a system claims to know, not just its output distribution) is directly applicable to any future ADM/hiring-algorithm advisory angle, and to sharpening Section 4 of the existing diagnostic for clients using AI in credit scoring, underwriting, or hiring — currently the template only scores documentation/process transparency, not validity-of-claim transparency.
