# Advisory Retainer / SOW One-Pager — Template

**Type:** Consulting Deliverable Template
**Status:** Active
**Created:** 2026-07-15
**Use for:** Converting an assessed, conflict-cleared inbound opportunity into an ongoing advisory arrangement. This was the explicitly named gap in [[Consulting_Deliverable_Templates_Index]] — the missing fourth step after diagnostic/briefing/workshop engagements have established value and the conversation turns to a retainer.
**Candidates per [[Inbound_Pipeline_Index]]:** [[PrivacyWeave_Inbound]] (🟢 clean gate); [[AEGIS_OS_Inbound]] (non-defence scope only — see gate note below).

---

## Purpose

A one-page scope document that can be sent to a prospective retainer client without a full legal SOW drafting cycle — it fixes the commercial and scope boundaries before legal drafts the binding agreement, and it forces the scope/conflict discipline this vault already requires (per [[Inbound_Pipeline_Index]]'s operating rule: nothing progresses without a conflict clearance entry in `12_Decision_Journal`) into a single reusable document rather than an ad hoc email thread.

## 8. IP, Confidentiality, and Independence

- Client-specific findings, data, and names stay in the client deliverable only — never enter vault concept "Instances in Corpus" sections (those are reserved for published/public regulatory evidence, per existing convention in [[Governance_Diagnostic_Report_Template]]).
- Frameworks, checklists, and diagnostic instruments used in the engagement (e.g. this vault's consulting templates) remain the advisor's own IP and may be reused across clients; client-specific scoring and findings do not.
- State explicitly whether the advisory relationship itself may be disclosed publicly (e.g. in a future [[BFSI_AI_Governance_Handbook]] case study, per the disclosure precedent already noted in [[AEGIS_OS_Inbound]]'s Future Engagement Options table).
