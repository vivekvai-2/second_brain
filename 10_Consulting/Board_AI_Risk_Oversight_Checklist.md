# Board AI Risk Oversight Checklist — Template

**Type:** Consulting Deliverable Template (one-pager)
**Status:** Active
**Created:** 2026-07-15
**Use for:** The board-facing practitioner tool referenced in [[Executive_Board_Briefing_Deck_Template]] Section 4, and the board-level output of [[Governance_Diagnostic_Report_Template]] §5 and [[Agentic_AI_Security_Diagnostic_Template]] §5. Designed to be handed to a board or risk committee as a single page, not read as a report.
**Anchors on:** [[Accountability]], [[Governance_Capacity]], [[Institutional_Coherence]]

---

## Purpose

Boards asking "are we governing AI properly" rarely have a compact instrument to answer it with — they get either a 40-slide deck or nothing. This checklist compresses three independently-sourced governance-question sets — a multi-stakeholder cybersecurity framing ([[04_Knowledge_Products/Wiki_Intake/F072_WEF_Oxford_AI_Cybersecurity_Balancing_Risks_Rewards_2025|F072]], WEF/Oxford), a BFSI-industry-consortium board-accountability chain ([[04_Knowledge_Products/Wiki_Intake/MindForge_MAS_AI_Risk_Management_Executive_Handbook|F054]], MindForge), and the Indian central-bank principles framing ([[07_Institutions/Corpus/RBI/RBI_FREE_AI_2025|RBI FREE-AI]]) — into a single page a board can actually use in a 20-minute agenda item.

---

## The Checklist

| # | Question | Why It Matters | Red Flag If... |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Has the board approved a formal AI Risk Appetite Statement, distinct from the general risk appetite statement? | Per MindForge C4 — quantified where possible (e.g., acceptable false-positive rate for a fraud model) | No AI-specific statement exists; AI risk is folded silently into general operational risk appetite |
| 2 | Is AI governance a standing board or board-committee agenda item, with senior management reporting on AI risk materialisation? | Per MindForge C1 — board-level accountability, not delegated silently to IT/technology committee | AI governance has never appeared as a named board agenda item |
| 3 | Has the board established the organisation's risk tolerance for AI, and is it understood by all risk owners? | Per F072 (WEF/Oxford) — the foundational governance question; everything else is downstream of this | Risk tolerance is assumed, not documented or communicated |
| 4 | Are risks weighed against rewards for every new AI initiative before approval? | Per F072 — prevents adoption decisions being made on opportunity case alone | AI project approvals reference business case only, with no paired risk assessment |
| 5 | Is there an effective process to govern and track every AI deployment across the organisation — including which systems are agentic/autonomous? | Per F072 + [[Agentic_AI_Security_Diagnostic_Template]] §1 — "shadow AI"/"shadow agent" risk is the single most-cited emerging gap across the corpus | No central AI/agent inventory exists; business units may be running unapproved deployments |
| 6 | Is there board-level clarity on which internal stakeholders (legal, cyber, compliance, technology, risk, HR, ethics) must be involved in assessing and mitigating AI cyber risk? | Per F072 — cross-disciplinary risk function, not a single-owner model | AI risk ownership sits with one function (usually IT) with no cross-functional structure |
| 7 | Are assurance processes in place confirming AI deployments remain consistent with legal, regulatory, and internal-policy obligations — and can this be demonstrated to a regulator on request? | Per F072 + RBI FREE-AI Board-Approved AI Policy recommendation | No audit trail exists connecting a specific AI deployment to a specific board-level approval |
| 8 | Does the board have — or have access to — sufficient AI literacy to interrogate management's AI risk reporting, rather than simply receiving it? | Per MindForge C15 — Board and senior management AI literacy as a named capacity requirement, and the single most-cited [[Governance_Capacity]] gap in the Indian BFSI corpus | No director has documented AI/ML literacy; AI risk reporting is accepted without challenge |

## How to Use This With a Client

1. Do not send this as a form to fill in — walk the committee through it live, question by question, and note the answer plus evidence in real time. The value is in the conversation it forces, not the completed table.
2. Any question answered "no" or "we don't know" is a finding — log it, and cross-reference to the relevant section of [[Governance_Diagnostic_Report_Template]] or [[Agentic_AI_Security_Diagnostic_Template]] if a fuller diagnostic is warranted.
3. Three or more red flags = strong case for a full diagnostic engagement, not just an advisory conversation.
4. **RBI FREE-AI Seven Sutras caveat:** the Seven Sutras named in [[07_Institutions/Corpus/RBI/RBI_FREE_AI_2025]] (Trust, People First, Innovation over Restraint, Fairness & Equity, Accountability, Understandable by Design, Safety/Resilience/Sustainability) carry an open ⚠VERIFY flag on exact primary-text wording — reference the *principle* in client conversations, not a verbatim quotation, until that flag is closed.
