## What They Do

Offline Intelligence (offlineintelligence.io) is a US-based early-stage startup building sovereign AI infrastructure for data-sensitive and mission-critical environments. The core proposition is air-gapped, hardware-agnostic AI that operates with zero data egress — designed for legal, defence, and compliance-driven organisations that cannot use cloud AI due to regulatory mandates (GDPR, HIPAA, CMMC, EU AI Act) or operational constraints (denied/degraded environments, classified data).

**Architecture:** Open-source core (Apache 2.0), persistent memory, hardware-agnostic (x86, ARM, GPU, Jetson, edge), air-gapped and denied-environment ready.

**Dual-market strategy:** Legal AI as beachhead (proving compliance architecture; Offline Counsel product ready for pilots) → Defence/tactical as scale play (same architecture on tactical edge hardware).

**Business model:** Per-lifetime license ($3,999 for legal; $50K–$5M+ per defence contract); $3M seed raise targeting.

**India relevance:** DRDO and IDEX engagement explicitly mentioned in go-to-market Phase 2. Seeks a US-citizen co-founder for DoD pipeline; India defence track is separate but referenced.

**Founder:** Akhil Pamarthy — solo technical founder; all code designed, built, and tested by one engineer.
