# AGENTS.md

This file provides context about this Obsidian vault for AI agents.

## Vault Overview

The PRIS (Personal Regulatory Intelligence System) vault is a highly structured, academic-grade research, consulting production, and policy intelligence engine containing 413 active markdown files. It serves as a comprehensive knowledge base for analysing, synthesising, and operationalising regulatory frameworks, policy developments, and academic research across AI Governance, Digital Public Infrastructure (DPI), and Banking, Financial Services, and Insurance (BFSI) sectors. The vault functions as a self-reinforcing intellectual system where real-world consulting insights directly inform original conceptual frameworks, academic publications, and structured policy handbooks.

## Organization

The vault is meticulously structured around a core knowledge hierarchy (`Strategy` -> `Projects` -> `Knowledge Products` -> `Domains` -> `Concepts` -> `Frameworks` -> `Institutions` -> `Methods` -> `Evidence`) and is navigated using a robust Map of Content (MOC) system. Main folders include:

- **01_Strategy** (6 files): High-level roadmaps and publication pipelines (e.g., [[VV_Publication_Pipeline_v3]], `VV_Life_Roadmap_V2`), now featuring the `VV_Governance_Advisory/` subfolder (4 files) for managing advisory strategy and partner mapping.
- **02_Projects** (26 files): Active academic papers, thesis architectures, and research projects (e.g., `P2_DPI_JSIS`, `P3_BFSI_JEIM`, `P4_Doctrinal_IJLIT`, `P5_AI_Ethics`), utilizing a `References/` subfolder (5 files) and a structured `Shared_Anchors/` directory (12 files) containing foundational literature (e.g., Bovens 2007, Crawford & Ostrom 1995, Fiss 2011, DiMaggio & Powell 1983, Butler et al. 2023).
- **03_Domains** (6 files): High-level thematic syntheses including [[AI_Governance]], [[BFSI_Governance]], [[Cybersecurity_Governance]], [[DPI_Governance]], and [[Privacy_DataProtection]].

## User Preferences

The user demands an exceptionally high level of academic rigour, structural discipline, and formal precision. Analysis must avoid generic summaries, focusing instead on deep, actionable policy and research synthesis that complies with the vault's design principles.

Key operational protocols include:
- **Drafting Discipline Protocol (DDP)**: High-discipline drafting rules for academic papers, including locking the narrative spine first, placing the contribution claim in Section 1 before 500 words, and maintaining sections of at least 600 words for structural balance.
- **Continuous Compounding Loop**: Consulting engagements and client deliverables must feed back into conceptual notes, which in turn enrich academic projects and knowledge products.
- **Ontological Consistency**: Every new note must strictly conform to the established folder hierarchy and utilize the appropriate system template from `99_System_Templates` (e.g., Concept, Domain, Framework, Method, or Project templates).

## Custom Instructions

- **Strict Link Hygiene**: Never write `.md` inside `[[...]]` wikilinks. Standard Obsidian `[[Name]]` syntax must be used exclusively to avoid breaking links. Adhere strictly to the [[Bidirectional_Linking_Convention]].
- **Systematic Intake Processing**: All new regulatory reports, academic papers, or external guidelines must be ingested via the `04_Knowledge_Products/Wiki_Intake/` folder using the standard naming convention (e.g., `F[Number]_[Author]_[Topic]_[Year]`). Ensure the serialized `F[Number]` prefix is incremented correctly.
- **Institutional Corpus Categorisation**: Regulatory documents must be filed in their respective institutional subfolders within `07_Institutions/Corpus/[Institution]/` to keep the corpus organized.
- **Methodological Alignment**: When analysing institutional texts, align findings with [[Institutional_Grammar_IG2]] syntax (Attributes, Deontic, Aim, Conditions, Or-else) or [[fsQCA]] configurational logic where appropriate.
- **Workflow Discipline Protocol (WDP)**: All agents must strictly adhere to the three disciplines:
  - *Link Discipline:* No bare wikilinks; every link must have contextual text explaining its analytical relationship. Enforce bidirectional linking immediately by adding inward references to concept and MOC files in the same session.
  - *Concept Discipline:* Enforce the "Three-Instance" Gate. Never create a new standalone concept note in `05_Concepts/` unless it is observed in at least three distinct primary-source documents (corpus nodes) or is a direct, load-bearing theoretical lens in an active paper.
  - *Close-out Discipline:* Always reserve the final 15 minutes of a session for close-out activities (the Exit Clock). Every session must leave behind a "Compounding Deliverable" and generate a standardized session handoff note in `00_MOC/` (e.g., `Session_Handoff_Batch[Number]_Start` or specialized ingestion handoffs).
